# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be civil. Discuss the code, not the person.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space that
belongs to this project.

## Expected behaviour

- Say what you mean, plainly. Technical directness is welcome here; contempt is
  not.
- Assume the other person is trying to build something that works.
- Accept that a decision may be settled. Several are recorded as
  [ADRs](docs/20-architecture/adr/README.md), including the rejected
  alternatives, specifically so they do not have to be argued twice.
- Respect the safety rules. The [Send Guard](AGENTS.md#3-the-send-guard-is-not-negotiable)
  is not up for negotiation without a superseding ADR, and a change that weakens
  it will be declined regardless of how it is argued.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including phone numbers, message
  contents or session data pulled from a real Telegram account.
- Encouraging or facilitating spam, bulk messaging or any use of this project to
  contact people who have not consented to hear from the account owner. The
  project refuses to message strangers by design; asking for a way around that
  is out of scope, and advocating for it is out of order.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue, or privately through
[GitHub's report form](https://github.com/anrysys/tg-ai/security/advisories/new)
if it should not be public. Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions
that violate this document, and may block accounts that do so repeatedly.

## Attribution

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened deliberately: a code of conduct nobody reads protects nobody.
